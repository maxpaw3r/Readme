# AI Prompt Engineering & Visual QA

Hi, I'm Max. I'm a VFX Supervisor and Lead Compositor, and this repo is where I document my technical tests with generative AI models like Seedance 2.5 and ChatGPT 5.6 Sol.

Instead of just generating visually appealing concepts, I evaluate these models through the lens of a compositor. I audit spatial reasoning, temporal consistency, and lighting logic to find exactly where the AI breaks down—and determine what it actually takes to integrate these assets into a real production pipeline.
